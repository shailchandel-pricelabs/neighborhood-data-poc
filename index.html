Future Prices

Shows your listing's price compared to the market for upcoming dates. The solid line is your current price, while the shaded bands show the 25th–75th percentile range of comparable listings in your comp set.

Use the time range pills to adjust how far ahead you're looking. Tap the gear icon to overlay additional data like last year's bookings or market booked prices.

Occupancy

Tracks your listing's booked and unavailable nights alongside overall market occupancy. Blue bars show your booked nights (darker) and blocked/unavailable nights (lighter). Orange bars show market occupancy for your comp set.

Toggle "Last Year Data" and "Pickup" in the legend to compare with historical occupancy or see booking pace changes.

Market History

Shows historical market performance for your area over the past 1–2 years. Tap the metric cards (Occupancy, ADR, Booking Window, Length of Stay) to switch which metric the bar chart displays.

Use this to spot seasonal trends and year-over-year changes in your market.

Competitor Calendar

See what your competitors are charging on any given date. Swipe through dates to compare, and scroll the grid to browse all competitors. Tap "View all" to see every competitor in a single list.

Use the gear icon to switch between host and guest pricing, or to manage which competitors are in your set.
