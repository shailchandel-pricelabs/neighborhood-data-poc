How to read my prices?

The black line is your listing price. The shaded bands show daily rates of nearby listings over the next year: grey for the 25th–50th percentile, dark peach for 50th–75th and light peach for 75th–90th.

Purple bars mark events and holidays (daily view only).

Data: your compset from Nearby Listings, or a Market Dashboard if you use one. Read it as a trend, not exact market research.

How to read my occupancy?

The red line is the average occupancy of listings in your area for each date over the next year. Grey bars are your own unavailable nights.

Last Year Data adds occupancy on this day last year and how last year finally landed. Pickup shows bookings that entered the market in the last 7 days.

Data: your compset. Occupancy refreshes every 2 days.

What are market metrics?

Monthly market trends over the last 12 or 24 months.

Occupancy: average occupancy of listings in your area.
Booking Window: median days between booking and check-in.
ADR: median nightly price per booking.
Length of Stay: median nights per booking.

Note: from Sep 15, 2026, Airbnb rates include commission, which affects year-over-year comparisons.

How does it work?

Compare your nightly prices with up to 10 nearby listings. Greyed-out dates are unavailable; struck-off prices are the last price seen before the date was booked or blocked.

Switch to Guest Prices to see what a guest pays for a chosen length of stay, including fees.

Understanding Guest Prices

  • Prices are the average nightly rate for the selected length of stay (LOS), including all applicable fees.
  • For the total, multiply by the LOS. E.g. $120 average for a 3-night stay = $120 × 3 = $360.
  • N/B (non-bookable) means no price for that LOS. E.g. a date with a 3-night minimum shows N/B when 2 nights is selected.

Data: listings within 200 km with similar bedrooms on the same platform (Airbnb or Vrbo). Rates refresh every 5 days.
