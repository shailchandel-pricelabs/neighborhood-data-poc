Future Prices

Shows your listing's price compared to the market for upcoming dates. The solid line is your current price, while the shaded bands show the percentile range of comparable listings in your comp set. Switch to Monthly for a bar-chart summary with percentile lines, matching the desktop view.

Use the time range pills to adjust how far ahead you're looking. Tap the gear icon to overlay your own upcoming or last year's bookings on the chart.

Occupancy

Tracks market occupancy for your comp set against the same period last year. The salmon line (or bar) is current market occupancy; grey shows Last Year (Today) — where last year stood at this same point in its booking window — and Last Year (Final) — how it ultimately landed.

Green shows the 7-day Market Pickup pacing line, comparing how fast the market is booking up now vs. the same week last year. Use the gear icon to show or hide it.

Market History

Shows historical market performance for your area over the past 1–2 years. Tap the metric cards (Occupancy, ADR, Booking Window, Length of Stay) to switch which metric the bar chart displays.

Use this to spot seasonal trends and year-over-year changes in your market.

Competitor Calendar

See what your competitors are charging, in three views: Calendar browses a tile per competitor and swipes through dates, Table compares every competitor and date at once, and Map shows them geographically color-coded by price range — tap a pin for details.

Use the gear icon to switch between host and guest pricing, or tap "+ Add" to manage which competitors are in your set.
